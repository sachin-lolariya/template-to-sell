Frequently Asked Questions

Avengers: Doomsday is scheduled for global theatrical release on December 18, 2026. The live timer at the top of this portal tracks the exact seconds remaining until launch.

The film is directed by acclaimed MCU veterans Anthony and Joe Russo (directors of Infinity War and Endgame), with Robert Downey Jr. making a landmark return to star as the titular monarch, Victor von Doom / Doctor Doom.

Avengers: Doomsday (Dec 18, 2026) centers on the escalating multiversal incursion catastrophe and the ascension of Doctor Doom. Avengers: Secret Wars (Dec 17, 2027) is the grand finale of the Multiverse Saga, bringing together heroes across alternate realities onto Battleworld.

An incursion occurs when two alternate realities drift and collide at their central nexus points, inevitably threatening the mutual destruction of both universes unless one or both are stabilized.
