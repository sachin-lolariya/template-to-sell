Real habits we find in almost every first audit. Hover a card, or tap the button underneath, to flip it and see what we did instead.

  • The beforeNo. 01

    The essay graphic

    We are thrilled to announce our new range of sustainable solutions for the modern home.

    42 words on a beige square

    The fixNo. 01

    Your new favourite mug survives the dishwasher. We checked 300 times.

    One line, one photo, one proof point

    The lesson

    If it needs a paragraph, it is a caption, not a graphic.

  • The beforeNo. 02

    Logo first

    Logo spins for two seconds. Then a slow pan across the office.

    Lost 71% of viewers by second three

    The fixNo. 02

    Open on the result. A packed shop on a Tuesday.

    Kept 2.4x more viewers at second five

    The lesson

    Nobody followed you for your logo animation.

    Two suits shaking hands in front of a window.

    Zero saves, one confused comment

    The fixNo. 03

    The actual team, mid-argument about a font.

    Most saved post of the quarter

    The lesson

    Real people beat pretend people every single time.

Requests

Search messages

  • How fast can you start?

    Seen
    U

    Usually within two weeks. The first week is a forecast: we read your accounts, your category and your audience, then come back with a plan. The second week we shoot.

  • Do you only do TikTok?

    Seen
    U

    No. We cut for wherever attention is cheapest for your brand: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and LinkedIn video. One shoot feeds four distribution engines.

  • Will it still sound like us?

    Seen
    U

    Always. We interview you and your customers on day one to build a voice bible. If a line feels like generic influencer marketing, it gets killed before filming.

  • What does a forecast actually include?

    Seen
    U

    A teardown of your current engagement, competitor vulnerability audits, 30 bespoke content concepts, and exact shooting scripts with hook variations.

  • Do we have to sign a long contract?

    Seen
    U

    Never. We work on month-to-month retainers. If the content isn't generating qualified reach and revenue after 60 days, you can walk away with zero friction.

  • Can we be in the videos?

    Seen
    U

    We prefer it. Founder-led content converts significantly higher than anonymous stock b-roll. We coach you through delivery until you look natural and authoritative on camera.
