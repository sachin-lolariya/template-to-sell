## 🎯 Executive Art Direction Philosophy

Aether Atelier exists at the convergence of **luxury high-fashion editorial**, **high-precision hardware engineering**, and **clinical biotechnology minimalism**. 
Every visual asset must feel like a \$50,000 commissioned commercial shoot from a top London or Milan creative agency (e.g., M/M Paris, Pentagram, Bureau Betak).

### Forbidden Visual Elements (Anti-Slop Directives)
- ❌ **No cheesy corporate stock gestures**: Handshakes, people smiling at laptops, thumbs up, generic whiteboard meetings.
- ❌ **No fake AI plastic skin or wax figures**: Avoid Midjourney's default oversaturated bloom, glossy airbrushing, or mutant extra fingers.
- ❌ **No generic neon cyberpunk lighting**: Replace purple/magenta neon with natural directional daylight, 4800K studio softboxes, or clinical cleanroom illumination.
- ❌ **No cluttered compositions**: Every frame adheres to intentional negative space, architectural balance, and strict focal hierarchies.

---

## 📐 5-Layer Structured Prompt Framework

Every prompt is constructed using the rigorous 5-layer taxonomy:
1. **Layer 1 // Subject Description**: Primary subject, micro-textures, tactile materials, and organic imperfections.
2. **Layer 2 // Environment & Setting**: Architectural context, spatial minimalism, and material backdrops (travertine, raw concrete, felt).
3. **Layer 3 // Lighting Specification**: Exact source (Profoto 5-foot octabox, low winter sun), direction, specular highlights, and color temperature.
4. **Layer 4 // Camera & Optics**: Medium format digital sensor (Hasselblad H6D-100c), focal length, aperture (`f/1.4`, `f/2.8`), and film grain.
5. **Layer 5 // Syntax & Platform Parameters**: Aspect ratio (`--ar 16:10`, `--ar 9:16`), `--style raw`, `--v 6.1`, `--c 0`.

## 🛠️ Implementation Guidelines for Website Deployment
When deploying images generated from these prompts into `images/`:
1. **Compress to Modern Formats**: Convert all outputs to WebP (quality 85) alongside progressive JPEG fallbacks.
2. **Explicit Dimensions**: Ensure all `<img>` tags specify `width`, `height`, and `loading="lazy"` (except the Hero LCP image which must use `loading="eager"`).
3. **Contrast Verification**: Ensure text overlays meet WCAG 2.1 AA (4.5:1 minimum) using semi-transparent gradient backdrops (`bg-gradient-to-t from-black/80 via-transparent to-black/30`).
