# Updraft Design System & Visual Tokens

A high-polish design system adhering to the `/design-taste-frontend` and `/awesome-design-md` standards.

## 🎨 Color Palette
- **Canvas / Primary Ground (`--cream`)**: `#FAF7F2` (Warm, editorial pearl off-white)
- **Elevated Surfaces (`--paper`)**: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp clean white cards)
- **Secondary Ground (`--sand`)**: `#F0ECE1` (Muted tactile sand)
- **Deep Ink (`--ink`)**: `#111317` (High-contrast obsidian espresso)
- **Signature Accents**:
  - Coral Ember: `#FF4405`
  - Electric Citron: `#E2FD52`
  - Cobalt Focus: `#1E40AF`

## 🖋️ Typography Scale
- **Sans Display / Body**: `Inter, sans-serif`
- **Headings & Editorial**: `Magra, sans-serif`
- **Expressive Serifs**: `Instrument Serif, serif`
- **Telemetry & Labels**: `DM Mono, monospace`

## ⚡ Motion & Micro-Interactions
- **GSAP 3 Timelines**: Easing curves `power3.out` and `power2.out`.
- **Card Hover**: 3px vertical lift with shadow bloom.
- **Section Transitions**: Smooth ScrollTrigger fade-in on enter.
